Door Scanner Handbook

How to check people in with your phone. Ten minutes to read, and worth reading before the night rather than during it.

If you only read one thing

  1. Sign in at /scan before you get to the venue, on wifi.
  2. Open your show once while you still have signal, so the guest list downloads to the phone.
  3. Press Start scanning. This turns on the camera and the sounds.
  4. Green means let them in. Amber and red mean stop and read — but you can always let a real guest in anyway.
  5. If a family shows one phone, tap Admit remaining so all of them are counted.

Before the night

Five minutes at home beats five minutes with a queue

Your admin will give you two things: a display name and an access code. Not an email address — you will never need one for this.

  1. Open /scan on the phone you will actually use Use that phone, not a different one. The sign-in stays on the device, so you only do this once.
  2. Type your display name exactly as it was given to you Spelling and spacing matter. If it doesn't work, it is nearly always this.
  3. Type the access code Let your phone save it. If you lose it, your admin issues a new one — nobody can look up the old one.
  4. Add the page to your home screen So you are not hunting through browser tabs in a dark foyer.
  5. Run the device check at /scan/diagnostics Two taps and three questions: does this phone buzz, does it beep, does the camera start? Better to find out now.
iPhones: two things to know

iPhones do not vibrate for this at all — that is the phone, not a fault. And the silent switch may mute the scan sounds. If both are off, colour is your only signal, which is exactly why the colours are so bright.

If the phone has no camera permission

The first time you press Start scanning the phone asks to use the camera. Say yes. If you tapped past it, the Search tab still works by name — and your phone's browser settings can give the permission back.

Setting up at the door

Do this while you still have signal

  1. Open the app and pick your show You will only be offered shows you have been assigned. If you have exactly one, it is chosen for you.
  2. Wait a moment for the guest list to download This is the important bit. Once it is on the phone, scanning works with no signal at all.
  3. Check the name at the top of the screen That grey pill is the show you are admitting. It stays there all night on purpose.
  4. Press Start scanning One press turns on both the camera and the sounds. Skip it and your first several scans are silent.
Check the show name before the first guest

With two nights of the same programme, an usher on the wrong show will reject every single valid ticket in front of them. One glance at the pill at the top costs you nothing and this costs you the evening.

The three tabs

Along the bottom, thumb-reachable

ScanThe camera. Where you spend the night.
SearchFind someone by name or code.
StatsHow many are in.

Switching tabs does not turn the camera off, so glancing at Search and coming straight back costs you nothing.

Scan

Hold the phone so the QR fills a good part of the screen. You do not press anything — it reads continuously. The same ticket held in front of the camera will not register twice in a row; that is deliberate.

Search

For the person whose phone is dead, whose printout is crumpled, or who says "I'm sure I'm on the list". Type a name or a ticket code.

Typing a ticket code

You do not need the whole thing. Type just the last few characters — three is enough. If the code on the paper reads ORC-7KTD, typing 7KTD finds it.

Tap the person to open their card, then admit them from there.

Stats

Four numbers: Admitted here, Remaining, Expected, and Awaiting sync.

These are your phone's numbers, not the hall's

If three of you are on the door, each phone counts only what it admitted until everything syncs. The desk has the real total. Don't panic at a low number on your own screen.

Awaiting sync is how many of your scans haven't reached the server yet. Any number here is fine; it clears itself when you have signal.

Refresh roster pulls the guest list again. Use it if the desk tells you someone was added late.

The five results

A colour, a sound, and a buzz. Read at arm's length.

The whole screen flashes for about a second, and the result stays up long enough to read. Green clears itself so you can keep moving. Amber and red stay put, because those are the ones with something to read.

Let them in Admitted

A valid ticket, first scan. Shows their name, their seat type, and — for a player's guest — who invited them.

Two rising beeps · one short buzz

Stop and read Already checked in

This exact QR was scanned before. It tells you when and by which usher. Usually a second family member showing the same PDF by mistake, or a double scan.

One flat beep · two short buzzes

Stop and read Wrong show

A real ticket, for a different night. The screen names the show it is actually for. Send them to the desk — politely; they have a genuine ticket.

Two falling beeps · one long buzz

Stop and read Cancelled · refer to desk

This booking was cancelled. The person and their seat were both real once, so this is a desk conversation, never a "you're not on the list".

Two falling beeps · one long buzz

Stop and read Invalid ticket

Nothing on your phone matches that code. Try the Search tab by name first — a wrong show downloaded, or a very late addition, both look like this.

Two falling beeps · one long buzz

Amber and red never block you

Every one of them offers Admit anyway. Press it when you believe the person in front of you. It is recorded with a note so the desk can look at it later.

Turning away a genuine guest at a free concert is worse than an extra row in a log. When you are unsure and there is a queue: let them in and tell the desk.

The speaker icon at the top mutes and unmutes the sounds, and the phone remembers your choice. Foyers are loud — sound helps, but the colour is always the real signal.

Families and groups

The single most useful button on the screen

A family of four received four tickets in one email. In practice the person at the front shows the first one and the other three walk in behind them.

When you scan a ticket that belongs to a party, the green screen tells you so:

Admitted ALICE TAN

Party of 4 · 1 in, 3 remaining

→ tap "Admit remaining 3"

One tap admits the other three. You don't have to ask a family to queue past you one at a time.

Please use it

If you scan one and wave the rest through without tapping, three people are inside and unrecorded — and the arrivals count is wrong for the rest of the night. That is the number the desk uses to decide when to stop holding seats.

A party that genuinely splits up — one arrives early, three arrive late — just scans individually. Nothing is forced. And a single guest never sees this button at all.

When there's no signal

Venue wifi is unreliable. This is designed for that.

Keep scanning. Everything works: the camera, the colours, the search, the party admissions. Your phone checks tickets against the list it downloaded, not against the internet.

A small badge appears at the top showing Offline or how many scans are queued. Both are normal. The moment signal returns, the queue sends itself — with the original times, so the arrivals graph stays honest.

The one banner you must not ignore

If you see "This device will not save your queue", the phone is almost certainly in private / incognito browsing. Scanning still works, but do not close that tab until everything has synced — the queue is not saved anywhere.

The fix is to reopen /scan in a normal window and sign in again.

Two phones, one family

If two ushers scan the same person while both are offline, neither phone can know. The server accepts both when they sync and flags it for the desk. Nothing is lost and nobody is turned away.

If something goes wrong

Most of it, you can fix standing where you are

What's happeningDo this
Sign-in won't accept your details Check the display name letter for letter — it is the usual cause. If the code is definitely right, ask the desk to issue a new one.
"No shows assigned yet" The admin hasn't ticked your show. One message to the desk fixes it; you don't need to sign in again.
The camera won't start Press Start scanning first. If it still won't, allow camera access in your phone's browser settings. Meanwhile, work off the Search tab.
No sound at all Check the speaker icon at the top, then your phone's silent switch. On iPhone the switch can mute it. Colour still tells you everything.
QR won't read More light, less glare, hold steadier, move a little further back. Off a phone screen, ask them to turn their brightness up. Then fall back to Search by name.
Everyone is scanning "Wrong show" Stop. Look at the pill at the top — you are on the wrong show. Tap it to switch, or ask the desk to assign you.
Someone is genuinely not on the list Desk. They have the full registration system and can add a person in under a minute.
You admitted the wrong person Tell the desk. They can revert a single scan from the dashboard. Don't try to fix it on the phone.
Your phone dies Anything already synced is safe. Anything queued on that phone is lost. Carry a battery pack; you are running a camera for an hour.
Signed out mid-shift Sign in again with the same name and code. If it refuses, your account was deactivated — desk.

That list is short on purpose. You are holding the full guest list for one show on a phone in a public foyer, so it carries names and ticket status and nothing else.

Door checklist

Run through this as you take your position

  • Signed in, and signed in on this phone Do it at home, on wifi.
  • Correct show showing in the pill at the top The single most damaging mistake, and the easiest to check.
  • Guest list downloaded while you had signal Open the show once online. Then signal stops mattering.
  • Pressed Start scanning Turns on the camera and the sounds together.
  • Scanned one real ticket as a test Ideally a printed one, so you know paper works in this light.
  • Battery pack in your pocket An hour of camera empties a phone faster than you expect.
  • You know where the desk is Every red result ends there.
